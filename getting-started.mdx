## Prerequisites

Before diving in, ensure you have:

<Callout kind="info">
- A modern web browser like Chrome, Firefox, or Safari
- An active Zwift account (if required for certain teams)
- Access to `https://zwift.community`
</Callout>

No downloads or installations needed—this is a fully web-based experience.

## Complete Setup in 4 Steps

Follow these steps to create your account, set up your profile, find teams, and register for events.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Your Account" icon="user-plus">
    Visit `https://zwift.community` and click **Sign Up**.

    Fill in your email, create a strong password, and verify your email address.

    ```
    Email: your.email@example.com
    Password: (choose a secure one)
    ```

    <Callout kind="tip">
      Use a password manager for secure storage.
    </Callout>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Complete Profile Setup" icon="settings">
    Navigate to your profile dashboard after login.

    Add details like your Zwift username, preferred discipline (cycling or running), location, and experience level.

    Upload a profile picture and set your availability for events.

    Save changes to make your profile discoverable by teams.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Browse Available Teams" icon="users">
    Go to the **Teams** section from the main navigation.

    Use filters for discipline, skill level, location, or activity type.

    View team descriptions, member lists, and upcoming activities.

    Click **Join Request** on teams that match your interests.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Register for Your First Event" icon="calendar">
    Head to the **Events** page.

    Browse upcoming community events like races, group rides, or social meetups.

    Select an event, review details, and click **Register**.

    Confirm your registration via email and prepare in Zwift.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Can't find suitable teams?" default-open="true">
    Try broadening your filters or checking different disciplines. Teams update frequently—refresh the page or contact support via the help icon.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Registration not confirmed?">
    Check your spam folder for the confirmation email. If issues persist, use the **Resend** button in your event dashboard.
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Profile not visible to teams?">
    Ensure your profile is public and includes required fields like Zwift ID. Privacy settings may block visibility.
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>
