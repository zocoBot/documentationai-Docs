---
title: Cannot Connect OWL.BiKe to Zwift Community
description: Quick fix for device connection issues.
---

## Symptoms
- Unable to link OWL.BiKe to Zwift Community
- "Connection failed" message

## Fix
<Steps>
  <Step title="Check WiFi">
    Ensure your OWL.BiKe is online and connected to WiFi.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Re-enter Credentials">
    Go to **Settings > Community**, disconnect, then connect again with correct details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Restart Device">
    Power cycle OWL.BiKe by turning it off and on.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="info">
If issues persist, contact support.
</Callout>
