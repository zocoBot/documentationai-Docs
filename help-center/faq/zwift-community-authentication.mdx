---

## Overview

Join Zwift Community groups using your OWL.BiKe credentials.

## Steps

1. Open the OWL.BiKe app
2. Go to **Settings > Community**
3. Select **Zwift Community**
4. Press **Connect**
5. Sign in with your Zwift Community account
6. Complete authentication

## Result

You can now access Zwift Community features and events on your OWL.BiKe device.
