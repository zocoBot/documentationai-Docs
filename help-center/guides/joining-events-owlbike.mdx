## Overview

Join community events through your OWL.BiKe device.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open OWL.BiKe">
    Power on your OWL.BiKe and launch the main menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Events">
    Go to **Community > Events** in the app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Event">
    Browse and select a Zwift Community event.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Join Event">
    Tap **Join** and confirm. Follow on-screen instructions for start time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
Arrive early for group rides or races!
</Callout>
